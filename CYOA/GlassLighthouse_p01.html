The Storm-Lashed Causeway

Salt spray stings your face as you cross the narrow basalt causeway, your oilskin coat snapping like a sail in the teeth of the Singing Gale. Ahead of you rises the Glass Lighthouse, a colossal spire of fused sea-quartz and blackened iron that has guarded the jagged shoals of World's Edge for three hundred years. Tonight, however, the great lantern at its crown is dark. Only an erratic pulse of violet static crawls across the quartz ribs, and the churning ocean is rising fast enough to swallow the stones behind your boots. In your gloved hand rests a heavy iron key etched with a crescent moon, pressed into your palm by the harbor master before your boat launched into the fog. All he told you was that the previous keeper, Vane, vanished three nights ago when the gale began to hum like a choir of drowned bells. As you reach the base of the tower, two paths present themselves through the sleet. Above the main threshold, the iron door stands ajar, revealing a spiral staircase where a faint, rhythmic chime echoes downward from the upper gallery. Near your feet, a rusted storm bulkhead has been pried half-open, leaking an eerie amber glow and the low grinding sound of submerged clockwork from the flooded cellar beneath the foundation.
